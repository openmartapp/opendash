# Using OpenDash

**English** | [Tiếng Việt](vi/user-guide.md)

This guide is for after setup ([`install.md`](install.md)). Names in **bold** are what OpenDash shows
on screen; on a phone set to Vietnamese, OpenDash is **Táp Lô** and Dash Player is **Kênh Xe**.

> Set things up while parked. On the move, use what the car screen and your voice offer, or let a
> passenger do it.

## Choosing a display mode

OpenDash › Settings (gear) › **Display mode**. Each mode keeps its own cells, so switching back
brings them back.

| Mode | What the car shows | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Direct** (default) | YouTube or the media library, full screen, with the speed and the next turn | Videos and music with the smoothest picture |
| **Built-in** | Up to 4 cells with OpenDash's views: websites, the media player, pages made for OpenDash | Several things at once without Shizuku |
| **Virtual displays** | Up to 4 cells with your installed apps | Your own apps side by side (needs Shizuku) |

## Direct mode: YouTube and the media library on the car

- **YouTube or the library:** the buttons at the top of the car screen show YouTube, the
  **Library** (albums, songs, videos, streams, favorites) or the **Streams**. One plays at a time:
  the other pauses, and the car's media card and keys follow the one shown. Every list starts with
  **Shuffle**. The library is managed on the phone, in the **Media library** card of the home
  screen (access to music and videos, streams and files, favorites); on the car you only pick.
- **Pick up where it stopped:** a video or song opened again goes on from where it stopped, with
  **Start over** for a few seconds.
- **Starts on what played last:** when the car connects, OpenDash opens on the last YouTube video
  or file from the library you played and plays it; a stream (a radio, a live link) is skipped
  for what played before it.
- **Favorites:** ☆ while something plays on the car; the library's **Favorites** tab plays them,
  and the ☆ at the top of the phone's home screen manages them.
- **Your YouTube account:** Settings › **YouTube account** › **Sign in** opens Google's sign-in
  page on the phone. Once signed in, YouTube on the car suggests videos for you, and the library
  gets a **YouTube** tab with your liked videos, Watch later, playlists, mixes and history: pick a
  list, then a video; the rest of the list plays after it. Videos played on the car go into your
  YouTube history. **Sign out** in the same place.
- **Next turn:** while Google Maps guides, its next turn shows beside the 🎤, read from its
  notification once you allow OpenDash to read notifications (nothing is stored). Settings ›
  **Next turn in Dash Player's card** shows it in the media card too.
- **Car screen:** Settings › **Car screen** chooses how OpenDash shows on Android Auto, from the
  next connection.
  - **Classic** (the default): OpenDash's own look. Directions open the navigation app on the
    phone (display over other apps needed). Once Google Maps has been opened on the car screen,
    its next turn no longer shows on OpenDash.
  - **Alongside Google Maps** (recent Android Auto): Google Maps keeps showing its next
    turn, and pops up each turn over OpenDash. Directions open Maps on the car screen, even with
    the phone locked, and the music plays on. A one-line bar at the top shows the next turn or
    what plays; OpenDash's buttons sit at the top beside it. OpenDash opens from Android Auto's
    list of apps. On wide screens Android Auto shows a Maps card beside OpenDash, which then
    cannot fill the screen.
  - With both, the voice button also works while Maps has the car screen: OpenDash listens without
    coming back ([voice commands](voice-commands.md)).

- **Choose what plays:** on the phone, search in **Direct: YouTube on the car**; on the car, tap 🔍
  at the top and type, or tap 🎤 and say it ([voice commands](voice-commands.md)).
- **Controls:** tap the video for OpenDash's buttons: play/pause, back or forward 10 seconds, next
  video, captions, reload, and which player to use. The car's media card and steering-wheel media
  keys work too when [Dash Player](#dash-player-the-cars-media-controls) is installed.
- **Speed:** the speed and the speed limit show over the video, from the sources in Settings ›
  **Speed sources** (phone GPS, OpenStreetMap, WazeMod). The speed turns red when it is over the
  limit. Settings › **Speed bubble size** makes it smaller or larger; it sits on the driver's side
  (Settings › **Driver on the right**). To move it, tap it on the car screen (it grows a little),
  then drag; it stays there. **Put it back on the driver's side**, under the bubble size, undoes
  that. Installing and connecting WazeMod: [`wazemod.md`](wazemod.md).
- **Player:** Settings › **YouTube player**. The YouTube website plays every video; the IFrame
  player is a plainer page, but videos their owners keep off other sites do not play in it.
- **Picture quality** follows the car screen: 480p on a screen under 720 pixels high, 720p up to
  1440, 1080p above. It keeps the phone cooler; a slow connection can still lower it. If the phone
  gets hot or the video stutters, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
- **Sound behind other apps:** Settings › **Play on behind other apps** keeps the sound going
  while a map has the car screen; the media card controls it. Off by default.
- Ads play as YouTube shows them, and OpenDash offers to skip one when YouTube does. In the paid
  version, Settings › **Skip YouTube's ads** (off by default) leaves them out. YouTube's terms do
  not allow that, and YouTube may warn your Google account or stop playing videos: it is your
  choice. Nothing changes for a YouTube Premium account, which has no ads.

## Cells: built-in and virtual-display modes

**On the phone**

- The preview shows the car's frame. Tap a cell to choose what it shows: **Apps** (virtual
  displays) or **Websites & media** (built-in: popular and your own websites, the media library,
  streams and files you added).
- Pick a layout: **Full**, **Side by side**, **Stacked**, **1 + 2** or **2 × 2**.
- Drag the grey handle on the line between two cells to resize them; drag past a third of the
  screen to hide one ("Release to hide"), and drag it back from the edge to show it again.
- Tap the heart next to an app to make it a favorite: favorites come first, on the phone and on
  the car.

**On the car**

- Tap a cell to select it (blue outline). Drags, scrolls and swipes go to the selected cell;
  pinches go where they are.
- The round OpenDash button on the line between cells opens **Layout**, **Back**, **Apps**
  (**Choose** in built-in mode), **Reload** (built-in) and **Info**. It closes again after 10
  seconds. Android Auto also shows an OpenDash button in its top corner that opens the same menu.
- Tap a resize handle to move the line to a third, half or two thirds of the screen, or to give
  one cell the whole screen.
- **Info** shows the screen area and lets you set up **Car buttons**.

## Buttons on the car, the phone or a remote

Android Auto keeps the car's arrow keys and rotary knob for itself, so OpenDash offers its own:
extra buttons at the top of the car screen, swipes and pinches, and keys on the phone or a
Bluetooth remote. Set them up in Settings › **Set up buttons** (or Info › **Car buttons** on the
car): choose a car (or **Any car**), tap **Learn a button**, press it, then pick the key it stands
for (Up, Down, Select, Back, Next cell, Menu, Play/pause, …). Keys on the phone or a remote also need
Settings › Accessibility › **OpenDash buttons** turned on; they act only while the car shows OpenDash.

With [Dash Player](#dash-player-the-cars-media-controls), the car's media keys and steering-wheel
buttons can be learned too. By default they drive the YouTube player: next video; previous starts
the video again, and a second press goes back to the one before; forward and back 10 seconds. Other
keys the car sends (stop, …) do nothing until learned; **Voice search** opens OpenDash's voice
commands, as the 🎤 button does. Search and assistant keys on a remote or headset open it too. The
car's own voice button stays with Google's assistant: learn another steering-wheel key for it.

## Dash Player: the car's media controls

Android Auto gives its media card, the steering-wheel media keys and the Assistant's "play … on …"
only to media apps. Dash Player is that media app for OpenDash: install `dashplayer-<version>.apk`
from the same release. It has no screen of its own. With it:

- The media card shows what OpenDash plays, with the speed and limit, and its buttons control it.
- The steering-wheel play/pause, next and previous keys control OpenDash's player.
- Android Auto's search in Dash Player, or "Hey Google, play jazz on Dash Player"
  ("phát nhạc jazz trên Kênh Xe" in Vietnamese), plays the first YouTube video found in OpenDash.

## Voice commands

Tap 🎤 at the top of the car screen and speak. Settings, the rules and how to change them:
[`voice-commands.md`](voice-commands.md).

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Display mode** | Direct, Built-in or Virtual displays (above) |
| **YouTube player** | YouTube website or IFrame player, for Direct mode |
| **Play on behind other apps** | Keep the video's sound while another app has the car screen |
| **Theme**, **Language** | The phone app's look and language (on Android 11 and 12 the car keeps the phone's language) |
| **Text and icon size** | Density of the apps in the cells |
| **Maximum frame rate** | Lower saves battery and keeps the phone cooler |
| **Let videos keep the phone screen on** | Off (recommended): the phone screen times out while videos keep playing on the car |
| **Buttons on the phone and remotes**, **Set up buttons** | See [Buttons](#buttons-on-the-car-the-phone-or-a-remote) |
| **Keep apps running with the screen off** | For phones that pause virtual displays when the screen turns off |
| **Favorite apps** | Apps shown first in the pickers |
| **Speed sources**, **Vehicle ahead** | Where the speed, limit and distance ahead come from, in order |
| **Speed bubble size** | Small, medium, large (default) or very large, over the YouTube video |
| **Voice commands** | The 🎤 button, its language, rules and app for directions |
| **Notifications** | OpenDash's "running" notification, with a Stop button; it goes away by itself about 10 seconds after Android Auto ends |
| **Export log** | Saves a log to send with a bug report ([`troubleshooting.md`](troubleshooting.md)) |
